1. The short version

  • We collect what we need to open your account, check your identity (as the law requires for buying gold and silver), take payments, hold your metal, hand it over at a dealer and pay you when you sell.
  • We don’t sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites. The app has no advertising or analytics tools.
  • Your app PIN is never stored on your phone. Our server keeps only a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of it.
  • You can see your records, correct your details and close your account in the app.

3. How we use it, and our legal basis

  • To provide the service you asked for: your account, purchases, holding and handing over metal, sales, gifts, appraisals and support.
  • To meet legal duties: identity checks, anti-money-laundering and record-keeping requirements in Pakistan [PGBX to confirm which laws and regulators apply].
  • To keep accounts safe: login protection, fraud checks and security notifications.
  • To tell you about your account: purchase, collection, payment and security notifications. You can switch off price alerts and phone notifications in the app.

We don’t use your data for marketing without asking you first, and we don’t make automated decisions about you that have legal effects without a person at PGBX being able to review them.

5. How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, we keep what we need to run it.
  • Records of purchases, payments, holdings, collections and sales are kept for [period the law requires] after the transaction, because the law requires it.
  • When you close your account, your login is ended on every device and your details are removed from the phone. After the legal retention period, your name, CNIC, date of birth, contact details, bank account numbers, chat messages and chat files are deleted, and the remaining transaction records no longer identify you.
  • Security logs are kept for [period].

6. How we protect it

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). On the phone, your login is kept in the iPhone Keychain or Android Keystore.
  • Your PIN is checked by our server and stored there only as a salted hash. After 5 wrong PINs the session ends.
  • The app locks itself after 2 minutes without use or when you leave it, and hides its contents in the app switcher. On Android it also blocks screenshots and screen recording.
  • Records of money and metal can’t be edited or deleted, only added to. Every time PGBX staff open your details or a chat, it is recorded.
  • Staff sign in with a password and an authenticator code and see only what their role needs.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See your data: your wallet, transactions and statements are in the app (Account › Your data). For a full copy, contact us.
  • Correct it: edit your profile in the app, or contact us for details you can’t change there.
  • Close your account and delete your data: Account › Close account (or see how to delete your account), once nothing is pending (metal held, collections, payouts or orders). Data the law requires us to keep is kept as described in section 5.
  • Notifications: switch them off in the app or your phone’s settings.
  • Complain: contact us first; you can also complain to [the relevant authority in Pakistan].
