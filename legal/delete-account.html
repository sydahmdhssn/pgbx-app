Delete your PGBX account

PGBX: Pakistan Gold Bullion Exchange

In the app

  1. Open the PGBX app and log in.
  2. Go to Account › Close account.
  3. Confirm with your PIN.

You can close your account once you hold no metal and nothing is pending (collections, payouts, orders, gifts or appraisals). Sell or collect your metal first. Closing ends your login on every device straight away.

Without the app

Call PGBX on +92 21 35215555 or WhatsApp +92 303 3521555 from the mobile number on your account and ask us to close it. We will confirm your identity before closing it.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted after the legal retention period: your name, CNIC number, date of birth, email, addresses, bank account numbers, rate chat messages and chat files, price alerts and phone notification settings.
  • Kept for [period the law requires]: records of purchases, payments, holdings, collections and sales, because the law requires them. After that they no longer identify you.
